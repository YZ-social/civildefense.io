The Civil Defense App

Identity without Accounts

There is no signup to use CivilDefense, and no phone number or email is asked for. Instead, each browser or installation automatically creates a cryptographically secured random identity, and maintains it until you remove it.

How Others See You

When you create or reply to an alert, the posting will be shown as "anonymous" with an automatically generated unique image.

If you like, you can change what will be shown for either of these by touching the "CD" button and suppling a different "handle" text or "avatar" image. The handle and avatar are how you choose to be recognized, do not have to be unique, and are shared with everyone.

How You See Others

You may find that you want to reliably recognize some contributors over time, even if their chosen handle and avatar are not unique, or if they change their handle or avatar between postings. You can do this by touching their avatar image, and choosing a handle or avatar by which you will recognize them. This choice is never seen by anyone else, so feel free to label them however you need.
